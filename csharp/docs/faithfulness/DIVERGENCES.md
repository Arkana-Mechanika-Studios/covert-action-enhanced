# Divergences register

Every place where the C# behaves differently from the original **on purpose**. Each entry has a
`// DIVERGENCE: D-n ...` comment at the place in the code; `TraceabilityTests` fails when a marker has no entry
here or an entry has no marker. Original bugs are not divergences: they are in `docs/original-bugs.md`
(project root) and handled through `OriginalBehaviour`.

A divergence needs a reason a player or a maintainer can check: the original's behaviour there is undefined,
unreachable, or bound to DOS. "It is simpler" is not a reason.

Not listed one by one, because it is a rule of the whole rewrite (ARCHITECTURE.md, "Out of the typed model"): an
index or a copy that leaves its table or buffer throws in the C#, where the original reads or writes the
neighbouring memory. Real play does not reach such a place; where it does, the neighbouring memory is modelled.
